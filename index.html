T&C FARM

Established 2020

OUR STORY

T&C Farm was born in 2020, an unlikely time to start something new. When the world slowed down, our family saw an opportunity to grow, quite literally. What began as a leap of faith during one of the hardest years in recent memory quickly took root into something we are deeply proud of.

But our story runs deeper than 2020. Flowers have been a part of our family for decades. Family members have been running their own floral business since 1995, and growing up in that world shaped everything that goes into T&C Farm. This is the continuation of that love, grown here in the Pacific Northwest by a family that has always believed in building something beautiful.

WHO WE ARE

"We wake up early, get our hands dirty, and pour care into every stem we grow."

A small family farm, built from the ground up. Every stem grown here reflects the hard work and heart that goes into everything T&C Farm does. Founded by two immigrants who carried a deep love for the land across oceans, this farm is proof that something beautiful can grow from dedication, patience, and a whole lot of early mornings.

Find us at Pike Place Market in Seattle, where we bring whatever is in season straight from the farm.

OUR HISTORY

T&C Farm is part of a much larger story. After the Vietnam War, thousands of Hmong families resettled across the United States, many finding their way to the Pacific Northwest. For these families, farming was more than a way to make a living. It was a way to stay rooted, to preserve culture, and to rebuild community in a new land.

Our farm carries that history forward. Founded by two immigrants who brought with them a love for the earth and the knowledge of what it means to build something from nothing, this farm is rooted in that legacy. Every season, every harvest, and every bouquet is a small continuation of something that started long before this farm existed. That is what T&C Farm is built on.

FROM OUR FIELDS

We grow seasonal flowers, which means what we offer changes with the time of year. Everything we sell comes straight from our fields to you. We don't source from wholesalers or outside growers, which means what's available depends entirely on what's blooming that week.

SPRING

Tulips and daffodils are among the first signs of life on the farm, filling the fields with color after a long winter.

LATE SPRING

Peonies take center stage, lush and fragrant at their peak for just a few short weeks.

SUMMER INTO FALL

The fields come alive with lilies, dahlias, sunflowers, and so much more. This is our most abundant season, with new varieties coming into bloom week after week.

AFTER FALL

When the winter frost settles over the farm and the last fresh blooms fade, we craft dried flower bouquets from what the farm has given us. A way to hold onto the beauty of the year a little longer.
